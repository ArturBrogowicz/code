## What it does (design table, P2-1; AP-8 rebuilt by the K chain)
1. **The packet's pre-checks:** a clean tree at the pin and its frozen hashes, the host (kernel, memory, CPU, VirtualBox version),
   uptime, chrony, timers, jobs, no testbed left, quiet.
2. **A speed hold against the record's route costs:** -10 % to +10 %, two pairs in a row, at most 30 min.
3. **`M_xdp`, seed 1:** the cheap flood of HG4 at level 8 against Nginx's limits plus control_plane.py banning into XDP.
   - 3 attack streams carry 5 sources at about 119.3 req/s each, 45 s from T = 30 s.
   - 29 legit streams run for 105 s; the pins are server 0-1, daemon 2, executor 6, legit 7, attack 3-5.
4. **`M_limits`, seed 1:** the same with Nginx's limits only, no daemon.

Outputs go to `/var/tmp/ddos_campaigns/hg4_mvp_xdp_recal.smoke/{M_xdp,M_limits,packets}`. The packet then writes a tar, its
sha256 and a one-line summary.

**It is not a result.** There is no control or unprotected run beside it, and one run per arm says nothing about harm or
protection. It checks that the real daemon starts, bans the attack and only the attack, and stops cleanly on this kernel at this
pin. It also checks that Nginx's limits act, before about 2 h of campaign (P2-2) depend on them.

**The drift check (prereg 6) is not needed for P2-1.** That is my reading of the prereg: its rule is "before a campaign of family f
that does not start in R4's window", and P2-1 is two smoke runs, not a campaign. P2-2 is the campaign; its start is a separate
question, put to you before P2-2.

## Side effects
- **The testbed:** namespaces server_ns and hg_0..hg_3 (205 addresses), the bridge and veth pairs. Nginx and gunicorn run in
  server_ns as root, and there are 32 engine processes.
- **The daemon (M_xdp only):** control_plane.py runs as root in server_ns. It compiles xdp_ddos_filter.c with BCC, imports torch and
  attaches XDP to veth_server. It is stopped with SIGINT, which detaches XDP, and the teardown checks that no program or map is left.
- **Host files:** `/var/log/nginx/ddos_ml_access.log` and `error.log` are moved into the run (`M_xdp/feed_before/`) and made anew
  by Nginx; they keep that run's lines afterwards. The self-signed certificate exists already, so none is created.
- **Outputs:** root-owned, under /var/tmp/ddos_campaigns.

Nothing is pushed. No package, kernel, timer, network or system setting is changed.
