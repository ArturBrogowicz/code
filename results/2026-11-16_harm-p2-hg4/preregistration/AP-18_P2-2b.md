## Why
P2-2 (AP-17) ended INVALID by its own rule (RES-14): 3 runs were unsound because the attack began 0.51-0.52 s early (limit 0.5 s),
and 4 valid blocks could not reach 10. Cause (C-159): the attack was launched on a fixed delay that assumes the legit engines take 2.9 s
to start; they took 0.2-0.5 s longer. You chose "Fix launch, then rerun" (D-114).

**K23, what changed (docs/HG1C_DESIGN.md departure 42):**
- The executor now sees each legit engine start its loop (its epoll descriptor under /proc). It takes the median of those starts,
  adds the engine's fixed 0.05 s from loop to schedule, and launches the attack 29.85 s after that. So the attack starts at T = 30 s
  whatever the start-up takes.
- Each run records how the attack was launched, any late look by the executor, and how far the post-run estimate fell from the
  watch's zero.
- Two independent reviews: no HIGH finding. The first review caught the 0.05 s frame error, which the simulator had hidden, and
  it is fixed (C-160).
- **Fallback:** if fewer than a majority are seen in time, it uses the old fixed delay.
- **Unchanged:** the engine, the record, the spec, the limits (the 0.5 s check still judges every run) and every rule on a
  measurement.
- **Tests:** on the simulated host with P2-2's slow start-up, the old launch leaves the attack 0.54 s early (unsound); K23 brings it
  within 0.05 s.
