## What it does (design P2-2; AP-9 rebuilt by the K chain)
**The campaign:** `bench/gate_campaign.py run bench/scenarios/cheap_static_flood_hg4.json --calibration <record> --modes xdp --out
/var/tmp/ddos_campaigns/hg4_mvp_xdp_recal --stop-after <the packet's minutes left>`.
- One unscored warm-up control at seed 1.
- 10 blocks, seeds 1-10. Each block is C (the control), Cp (the placebo, a repeat of C), U (the attack, unprotected) and M_xdp
  (the attack, with limits plus the daemon banning into XDP), in a drawn order. That is 41 runs of about 150 s.
- A failed block is made once more with its seed and a new order, at most 2 such re-runs in the campaign.
- **Every run is host-checked** (H1 speed bracket, H3-H7 from the monitors). A host-invalid run is kept, published and made again
  in place after a speed hold. 3 in a row or 4 in a packet pause it ("host unfit", exit 5).
- Each packet: the pre-checks, a speed hold against the record, then the campaign until 85 min of the packet are used (exit 4,
  "paused"). The same command resumes it.

## The reading (pre-registered in code: harm_gate.P2_OUTCOME, eval/harm_gate.py; read only after the campaign ends, rule R-2)
- **The gate:** needs 10 valid blocks; the floors are 10 pp and 1 in log2 p95; the interval 0.95; the same sign in 80 %. It gives
  PASS, FAIL or INVALID.
- **The P2 outcome for M_xdp:**
  - "unprotected fails": in 90 % of the blocks, dQ >= 10 pp or p95 >= 2 x C;
  - "protected works": Q(M) >= 0.99 x Q(C) in as many, **and no legit identity is banned in any M_xdp attempt**, valid or not.
  - It reads MET or NOT MET. The time to mitigate is reported, not judged.
- **Known before any data (T-092, D-086):** with the 0.99 ratio, NOT MET is a live outcome even with a daemon that works as designed.
  - The ratio allows the protected arm to lose 1 pp of the 45-s attack window.
  - The rule cannot ban before about 1 s; P2-1's time to mitigate was a median of 0.82 s.
  - You kept the ratio unchanged on 2026-11-11 (D-086). Changing it now would be a new pre-registration.

2. **The P2 rules you approved for AP-9 on 2026-11-11 (D-086) are carried to this rebuilt campaign:**
   - **The 0.99 ratio:** unchanged; it is in harm_gate.P2_OUTCOME at the pin.
   - **R-1, repair after a reset:** kept. Its main cause, files left empty by a reset, is now fixed in code: the executor's

     watchdog is needed.
   - **R-5, after INVALID:** at most one repeat campaign in a new directory, only for environmental reasons, decided before any
     arm figure of the invalid one is looked at; never after FAIL or NOT MET.

After your yes, the orchestrator:

   directory absent;
2. starts in the background
   `tasks/T-123/p22_launch.sh AP-17 688594a74093464ea69cd3ddb03da8afd528265c bench/calibration/ddos-mitigation-2026-11-16.json 3`
   (sha256 2a7a5f07...). That is rec_launch.sh for P2-2. It waits 180 s and runs
   `sudo bench/packets/packet.sh P2-2 --commit <pin> --record <record>`. **When a packet ends "paused (exit 4)" by its budget it

**To stop:** run `sudo kill -TERM <the stage's pid>` (in the launcher's log `tasks/T-123/p22_launch_<UTC>.log`), then
`pkill -f p22_launch.sh` so that no further packet starts. The same packet command resumes later.
