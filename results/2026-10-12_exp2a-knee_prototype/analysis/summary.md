## Notes

* Availability counts every legit request by the period in which it *started* (curl `--max-time 10`); a timeout counts as a failure.
* Confidence intervals resample whole runs; with few runs per mode they only reflect the spread of those runs. Fisher's test pools requests and ignores clustering by run and client.
* Time-to-mitigate is the first ban event of each bot minus the recorded attack start; false positives are ban events for legit clients (`ban events`), none by construction without a daemon (`no daemon`), or the legacy ping check (`ping`).
