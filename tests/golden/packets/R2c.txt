== packet R2c: one family's ladder (18 runs: 14 attack runs at 7 levels, 4 controls) ==
session     /S
budget      85 min: no run starts after it (--stop-after the minutes left), so the packet ends within 90 min
pre-checks  ./venv/bin/python -B bench/packets/prechecks.py --packet R2c --session /S --minutes 90 --commit COMMIT --record /R
            (clean tree at the pinned commit, and its frozen hashes: bench/, eval/, the testbed's files and the record hash to
            its blobs; kernel, MemTotal, CPU model and VirtualBox host version the session's, before its
            first step the laptop's (D-096), all four reported; uptime 1800 s, chrony within H4, no apt, dpkg or daily timer due
            within 90 + 10 min, no package job, no testbed left, no other process above 5 % of a CPU; each check fails
            closed on a command that failed or printed nothing)
detached    setsid nohup bash bench/packets/packet.sh R2c --stage ... > /S/packets/R2c_<UTC>.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &
step 1      ./venv/bin/python -B bench/hostcheck.py speed-hold --calibration bench/calibration/placeholder.json --reference /S/origin/origin.json --lo -0.10 --hi 0.10 --max-min 30
step 2      ./venv/bin/python -B bench/calibrate.py ladder --family costly_search_flood --out /S --stop-after REMAINING
after       tar -czf /S/packets/R2c_<UTC>.tar.gz /S; sha256sum of it into R2c_<UTC>.sha256
stop        kill -TERM <the stage's pid, printed at the start>: the TERM goes on to the step's process, the stage waits for
            its teardown, starts no further step and still writes the tar, the sha256 and the summary; the same command resumes
summary     one line at the end of the log: PACKET R2c <done|paused|host unfit|not frozen|stopped|stopped by TERM> (exit N):
            runs made, host-invalid. not frozen (exit 7) is a step's own rule (prereg 6); stopped (2) is a refusal of the host
            or the environment, unsound twice or a defect: no reading, the log's last error line says which
